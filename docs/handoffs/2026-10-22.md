## Gotchas

- Panako's native transform (libjgaborator) segfaults now and then while storing, even on 4 threads. A store resumes cleanly from the files not yet stored: collect stored paths from the store logs ("Stored N fingerprints for '<path>'"), subtract from `data/h2h/refs.txt`, and pass the rest with `eval.h2h index-panako --list`. Six or seven files always report "already stored"; they are in the index.
- Panako sometimes answers "metadata unavailable!" instead of a path (records lost in a crash); `eval.h2h` names those matches from its resource id.
- Forked multiprocessing workers deadlock once librosa has started threads; spawn them.
- The shell tool stops foreground commands at 10 minutes; run long jobs in the background.
- The Mac sleeps on battery when left alone, freezing every process; real-time harnesses hold caffeinate and flag stalls.
