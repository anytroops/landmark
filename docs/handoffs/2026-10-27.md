## Gotchas

- Timing runs: macOS background work (Spotlight, `mediaanalysisd`, XProtect scans) can hold a core for an hour after many files are written, and the battery sometimes charges from 80% to full first; wait for "not charging" or "charged" and an idle machine.
- Live capacity depends on free memory: with about 12 GB in use by other applications the laptop compresses memory from about 88 stations on. Close other applications for a fair comparison, and compare runs made in the same state.
- The live harness's stall check (`valid` in result.json: no gap over 3 hops between a stream's windows) also trips when a worker's 64-window buffer per stream fills and it stops reading audio; that is an overloaded worker, not a sleeping machine.
- `python -m bench.scripts.live rates <run dirs>` prints windows ready and answered per second, the clearest sign of a worker past capacity.
