## Gotchas
- Benchmark scripts refuse battery power and uncommitted code; never commit while a run is in progress (each point records HEAD at finish).
- The Mac sleeps with the lid closed even under caffeinate on battery; long runs need AC and the lid open.
- zsh: no word splitting of `$var` commands, unmatched globs abort `&&` chains, `pgrep -f` matches its own wait loop.
- Never gate a commit on a pipe (`make test | tail`): the pipe hides the failure.
- `kill -9` on a Docker container makes requests hang (address vanishes) rather than fail fast.
